2. File format

One row per overtime entry, five columns, header on row 1:

Working dateEmployee IDWorked InOvertime typeOT hours
2026-06-181042MARSRegular OT02:30:00
  • Worked In is the team this overtime was actually worked for — some departments pay a different rate per team. Leave it blank to default to the employee's own team on file.
  • OT hours as HH:MM:SS. A department paying a flat amount per day still needs any non-zero duration — the flat rate is resolved at calculation time, not from the hours.
  • Re-uploading the same employee/date/type corrects the earlier figure rather than duplicating it.
  • You can only upload rows for employees in your managed scope; others are rejected per-row.
  • Rows dated before the open payroll period, dated more than a year in the future or the past, or belonging to an already-paid period are rejected per-row and listed in the result.
  • Every row lands as Pending review. A manager (APM/PM or above) reviews it and the department head (or a department additional manager) approves it before it is paid. Re-uploading a reviewed or approved entry with different figures sends it back to Pending review; the same figures leave the sign-off in place.
  • A row landing in the most recently locked period is still accepted; once approved, the run must be recalculated before it's paid.
