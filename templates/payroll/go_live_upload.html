Upload go-live

Move a Data Solutions trainee to their new team, dated the real go-live day. The system prices the salary revision and sends it to Finance — you don't enter an amount.

← Back to Upload Center

1. Download a template

Excel template Blank rows for the three columns below, plus a reference sheet of your managed trainees' IDs and names.

2. File format

One row per employee, three columns, header on row 1:

Employee IDTeamGo-live Date
55043MARS2026-09-07
  • There is no amount column. It's resolved from the team's configured salary package (MARS is 18,000 Tk; every other team is 16,000 Tk), so it can never disagree with the seeded policy.
  • Trainee to Live only. An employee who isn't currently a Trainee is rejected — this uploader is specifically the go-live transition. A correction after the fact goes through the employee's profile.
  • Use the real go-live date, even if you're uploading it a few days later — the salary revision is dated from it, not from today.
  • The team and status change take effect immediately. The resulting salary revision, like every salary change, is Pending until Finance approves it — the employee keeps their current pay until then.
  • If the employee has no salary on file to revise, the team/status change still applies, but no revision is proposed — the report will say so.
