When a salary is revised back to an earlier date (the January increment first paid in August, or a later revision of it), every month since is short by the difference. Orbis derives that balance per employee from the compensation master and what each month actually paid; you decide how many months of it this run settles. A revision tops up the months already settled automatically in the next instalment.
