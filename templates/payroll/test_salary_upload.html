1. Download a template

Excel template Blank rows for the two columns below, plus a reference sheet of your managed employees' IDs and names.

2. File format

One row per employee, two columns, header on row 1:

Employee IDDays
350013
  • Days is the total for the whole period — if someone fell under test across more than one stretch of the month, add them together and enter one number. Whole numbers from 1 to 31.
  • The number is taken as given — already-unpaid (LWP) days should be netted out before you enter it; the system doesn't check the roster.
  • The total is capped at the whole variable portion per person per period.
  • Trainees are rejected — they're on a flat package and never carry this deduction.
  • Re-uploading for the same employee replaces their day count for the period rather than adding to it — useful for a correction. An already-approved row is left alone.
  • Every row lands as Pending. The department head approves it, and the payroll run must then be recalculated, before it takes effect.
