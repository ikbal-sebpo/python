A one-time cutover: the payroll months of this tax year that were processed before Orbis went live, one row per employee per month: exactly what was paid — every pay component (earnings and deductions), TDS and net pay — the same shape Orbis exports for any month it ran itself.   • One row per employee per pre-Orbis payroll month (YYYY-MM, named for the month the period closes in — 2026-07 is 16 Jun – 15 Jul). A later month is refused: an Orbis month comes from its run.
  • The amount columns are every pay component Orbis knows — earnings first, then deductions (meal, loan, disciplinary, test salary) — generated from the component list, so a column Orbis doesn't recognise fails the whole file. Blank = 0. Whole taka; a decimal is accepted and kept.
  • Gross salary — the gross paid for the month as in your register, before TDS. Salary adjustment — pay belonging to another period paid in this one: an increment-arrear instalment (the 2026-09 payroll carried the first), or a held payment released later. Positive only; it's what makes the row add up to the register.
  • TDS deducted and Net pay are their own columns (net is optional, reference only).
  • Salary adjustment months — when the row pays a Salary adjustment, how many months of the owed balance that instalment settled (the 2026-09 payroll's first instalment settled 3). The engine starts the next instalment from there; a warning if left blank on such a row.
  • An exported month imports back as-is: its "Mobile & Internet reimbursement" and "Challan No" columns are reference and skipped.
  • Applying also rewrites each employee's two true-up openings (TDS deducted / additional pay) from their month rows — additional pay counts only the taxable earnings, never a deduction.
