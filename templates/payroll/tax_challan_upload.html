Tax challans

One row per treasury deposit of TDS — the challan number, date, bank and total as printed on the challan, and the payroll month it paid. Record every month's deposit here as it happens; the tax certificate's challan table is a read of these rows. A challan already in Orbis is corrected by its number, never duplicated. The tax year is the one the deposit date falls in.

2. File format

Challan NoDateBankTotal Challan AmountPayroll month (optional)Note

2627-002274843412027-01-14MDB9167blankBonus deposit
  • Bank — matched by name or short code against Payroll › Banks ("BankAsia PLC", "Bank Asia", "BAL" all resolve). An unknown bank is an error, not auto-created.
  • Total Challan Amount — the whole deposit, every employee's tax it carried.
  • Payroll month — YYYY-MM, the period the deposit paid (named for the month it closes in: 2026-10 is 16 Sep – 15 Oct). For a month Orbis ran, the challan is linked to that calculated run; if the run isn't calculated yet the challan is still recorded (a warning) and re-uploading later links it. For a pre-Orbis month it is recorded as the month the deposit paid.
  • Several challans may pay one month (the deposit can be split across banks); each employee's tax sits in exactly one of them. Which employees, and how much of each, is the next step — TDS by challan (per employee).
