Generate GH OT

Software Development and Creative Development earn overtime solely for working a government holiday. Pick a date that's already a seeded government holiday, with attendance already synced for it — this creates one GH OT entry per present employee (checked in, with enough office hours) who doesn't already have one for that date. Doesn't sync attendance itself, so run sync_attendance_punches --date <date> first for a date that hasn't been synced yet.

General BPO also earns overtime solely for working a government holiday, but presence and hours both come live from ttms.sebpo.net — no local sync step needed. This creates one GH OT entry per employee TTMS reports worked hours for, paid at their exact worked hours (not a flat grant), for anyone who doesn't already have one for that date.
