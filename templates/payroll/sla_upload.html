1. Download a template

Excel template One row per employee in your managed scope, names and IDs filled in — the SLA month is left blank for you to add only for the people who achieved it.

2. File format

Two columns, header on row 1. Any further columns (employee name, team) are ignored.

Employee IDSLA month
27372026-08
  • There is no amount column, and no Met SLA column. Only list the people who achieved it — a blank SLA month is an untouched row and is ignored, so it's not possible to accidentally credit someone by leaving their row alone. The amount is priced from the configured rate; if none is configured for someone's department, the row is skipped and listed — never written as zero.
  • SLA month is the month the target was met, not the month it's paid in. August's SLA is paid in the period that's currently open.
  • A missed earlier month can be claimed alongside the current one — add a second row with the earlier SLA month. The two are tracked separately, so a retro claim is never mistaken for a double payment.
  • Re-uploading the same employee and SLA month corrects the earlier row rather than duplicating it — unless it has already been approved or rejected, in which case it's left alone and listed.
  • You can only upload for employees in your managed scope; others are rejected per-row.
  • Every row lands as Pending. The department head approves it, and the payroll run must then be recalculated, before it is paid.
