Import pre-Orbis AL record

Restricted to HR. Stores each employee's Annual Leave position as HR calculated it at the Orbis cut-over date. It is a historical record for the Service Book only — it does not change anyone's live leave balance, and it is not shown on the profile or the balance page.

1. Download a template

Excel template Prefilled with every active employee's ID, name and joining date for reference. Fill the four figure columns from HR's AL calculation; leave a row blank to skip that employee.

2. File format

One row per employee, six columns in this order, header on row 1 (HR's own column titles are fine — columns are read by position):

Employee IDTill 20242025 AL Taken2025 AL Balance60 Cap Till 2025Note
10420401260
4917331203315 taken in 2025, 3 deducted from previous balance
  • The as-of date below is the year-end HR's figures are stated at (2025-12-31 for the first batch) — it must be a 31 December. The Service Book rolls the figures forward one calendar year at a time from there; Orbis's own leave ledger covers everything after it.
  • Employees with under 12 months' service at the as-of date, or who joined after it, are skipped and listed in the report — the policy allocates no Annual Leave before the first anniversary.
  • The capped balance must be 0–60. If it doesn't equal min(60, Till + Balance) the row is still stored as given, with a warning so HR can double-check.
  • Re-uploading an employee for the same as-of date replaces their record.
