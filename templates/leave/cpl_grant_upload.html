Upload Compensatory Leave grants

For departments whose CpL entitlement can't be auto-detected from the roster (e.g. Data Solutions & Due Diligence, which decides CpL vs. overtime per employee outside Orbis), or a one-time historical backfill for any other department. You can only upload for employees within your own managed scope — HR/Admins can upload for anyone.

1. Download a template

Excel template A blank 4-column workbook — fill in one row per employee/holiday earned.

2. File format

One row per CpL grant, four columns, header on row 1:

Employee IDWorking DateStatusConsumed Date
48502026-08-26CONSUMED2026-08-28
49772026-09-04OPEN
  • Working Date must be an active government holiday on file in Orbis.
  • Status is OPEN (earned, not yet taken) or CONSUMED — blank defaults to OPEN.
  • Consumed Date is required when Status is CONSUMED; it can be before or after the Working Date (some departments grant CpL in advance within the same week).
  • Idempotent per (employee, holiday) — re-uploading a row already imported is skipped, not duplicated.
  • There is no approval gate — your upload is final. Corrections after the fact go through a manual adjustment in the CpL register or Django admin.
