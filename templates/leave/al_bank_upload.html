Upload AL bank (historical opening balance)

Restricted to HR. One-time seed of each long-tenured employee's current Annual Leave balance from the HR file, up to the 60-day lifetime cap. This sets each employee's opening balance on their current AL cycle — it does not create leave usage or change anything else.

1. Download a template

Excel template Prefilled with every active employee's ID and name for reference. Fill in the balance only for employees who actually have a nonzero historical AL balance — leave the rest blank, an unedited row is skipped.

2. File format

One row per employee, two columns, header on row 1:

Employee IDCurrent AL Balance (days)
53960
  • Balance must be between 0 and 60 (the lifetime carry-forward cap).
  • This is a one-time import per employee — a second upload for an employee who already has an opening balance is skipped and reported, not applied. Corrections after the fact go through a manual adjustment in Django admin.
  • There is no approval gate — your upload is final.
